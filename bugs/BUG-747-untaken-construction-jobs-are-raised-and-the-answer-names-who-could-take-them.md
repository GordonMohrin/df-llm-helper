# BUG-747 Construction jobs nobody takes ("no builder took it: no cause readable") for months beside labor holders: the kernel raises them, the answer names the hands
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high (refuge hatch open 450+ days, projects stalled for a year)
Found: 2026-11-11, Run 16d i4 Pagedsculpture y386-y390 (refuge hatch / seal 95,94,141: "no builder took it: no cause readable (labor 8/10, items 1/0, in burrow, not walled in)" for 450+ days; cancel + new place + `bp.resume` did not help), Run 16b i2 Whirledrampart y435-y440 (Cabinet 137,171,137, Coffin 146,140,137, Trap 110,134,140, Craftsdwarfs 150,170,137, Bridge 112,131,140: the same line with labor 25/31 and 31/31; 29 unclaimed ConstructBuilding; the Carpenters "5 shops, 17 jobs (0 working)")
Module: `lua/dfllm/economy.lua` (`T.boost`, `M.hands`, `M.boost_days`, `M.build_state` `rec.short`, `M.inspect.workload` `data.boost`)

## Root cause
- Every cause the kernel can read was excluded in these jobs: not suspended, the labor held by 8 of 10 / 25 of 31 / 31 of 31, the item attached, the site inside the burrow, not walled in. What is left is DF's own job choice. `inspect workload` of the same hours says "0 of 31 adults without a job, 128 unclaimed jobs" (i2 cli.log:4318) and "0 of 14 adults without a job, 26 unclaimed jobs" (i4 cli.log:735): the labor holders are busy with other jobs and a construction job waits at the end of DF's list. DFHack's own `prioritize` doc says it: construction jobs "can get ignored indefinitely in busy forts", and the job flag `do_now` ("top priority") is the cure. The baseline `prioritize defaults` does not include `ConstructBuilding` (and must not, for every job).
- `economy.build_state` (`rec.short`, economy.lua about 12756) could only answer "no cause readable ... busy or no path", without saying who could have taken the job, whether they reach the site and what they do instead. The kernel never touched the job's priority.
- The player notes say "8 free idle adults"; that is the daily scan (`labor.why.free` 13 in i2's state.b.json). `inspect workload` shows 0 idle at the moments the jobs were read, so both are true at different times. The live cause of the busy state (hauling floods, hospital jobs) is not proven; the job type the holders are at is now named.

## Fix
- `T.boost` (new daily economy task after `jcheck`; `economy.boost_jobs` runs it for tests): one pass over the job list (at most `M.BOOST.scan`). A ConstructBuilding job or designation dig job (`DIG_JOBS`) with no worker and not suspended that stays so for `M.BOOST.days` (3) game days gets `job.flags.do_now = true`, the oldest first, at most `per_day` (4) a day and `max` (8) raised and still untaken at once (DFHack warns that too many consume the fort). Only in PEACE and RECOVERY (`HUNT_MODES`): no ALERT, SIEGE or BREACH, so no builder is sent toward a threat and no bridge, gate, refuge or seal code changed. The records `st.nb` (first seen untaken) and `st.nbs` (raised) are pruned when the job is gone. kern.log: "jobs: N untaken jobs raised to top priority (do_now) after 3 d without a worker (M raised and still untaken now): build ConstructBuilding x,y,z; ...".
- `M.hands(K, rec)`: the adult citizens (no squad) holding the building's labor, how many have no job and no explaining state, how many of those stand on the walk group of the site, what the others do (`busy` by job type). `build_state` answers "no builder took it: nothing blocks it (labor 8/10, items 1/0, in burrow, not walled in); 2 idle holders reach it, DF did not assign it (priority raised 12 d ago)", or "...; all 25 holders busy (11 at StoreItemInStockpile)", or the cause "no builder: 2 idle holders of the labor, none on its walk group". `rec.hands`, `rec.boost`, `rec.do_now` are set and `rec.line` carries the same tail. Without census data the old text stays.
- `inspect workload` `data.boost` {live, total, max, after_days, raised[<= 8: job, kind, x, y, z, days]} and the clause "N untaken jobs raised to top priority" when any.

## Live check
After a Kernel reload: kern.log "jobs: N untaken jobs raised to top priority" within 3 days of a project's pieces standing untaken; the project's `waits:` text names "2 idle holders reach it, DF did not assign it (priority raised N d ago)" or "all N holders busy (n at <job>)"; `inspect workload` `boost.raised`; the unclaimed count falls. If a raised job stays untaken for weeks the text says so, and the next suspects are the item (walk group, burrow) and the building's own access, not priority.
