# BUG-888 Food piles swallow every new empty barrel: the cap was a 14-day verdict, not a standing rule
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: Run 17 i1 / i2 / i3 / i4: every batch of barrels lifts drink for about a game year, then the same stall returns. i2 y522: 483 barrels, 40 'empty', 457 of 488 not empty, 433 of them in 56 plant/food piles whose summed limits (885) left room for 450 more; the one freeze fired at tick 210736512 and was given back 130 days later (cx_pile_days is 14); gamelog 'Needs empty food storage item' stayed 3000-3040 per half year across it.
Module: `lua/dfllm/economy.lua` (`M.pile_standing`, `M.cx_freeze`, `M.cx_expire`, `M.cx_end`, `brew_watch`), `lua/dfllm/act.lua` (`pile_barrels`, unchanged)

## Root cause
Not confirmed (i2, C1, C3 are the hypothesis, no live measurement shows the cap changes brewing): the haulers of the food piles fill each new empty barrel with plants (StoreItemInBarrel) until the pile's barrel limit is reached; the limit was only lowered while a stall verdict held, for at most 14 days, and only from the full economy run's `watch` task. After the time ran out or the reason faded the limits came back, the piles took the next batch of barrels, and the stall returned. The freeze is a one-shot reaction to a symptom; the cause (limits above what the piles need) is permanent.

## Fix
`M.pile_standing` (daily from `brew_watch`, before any early return): from the first time DF's own reason opens the cure gate (>= cx_min_n cancels 'Needs empty food storage item'), `p.pilestand = true` and every day `act.pile_barrels('freeze')` writes the limit of each food pile that holds barrels down to the barrels it holds (never up; follows the barrels down; a pile without a barrel and the drink piles are left alone so food always has a container and drink may use barrels; the drink emergency still freezes mixed piles too). No timer: `cx_expire` returns for a standing cap, `cx_end` refuses without `force`. It ends only through `M.stand_end`: the switch `economy.cancel.stand=false`, or no Still / no brewable plant for `stand_quit_d` (30) days; then the old limits are restored. Config keys `economy.cancel.stand`, `stand_quit_d`. kern.log: 'container stand: ...', 'container stand over: ...'.

## Tests
`tests/lua/test_bug887_891_fass.lua` (piles 40/30 limits holding 10/6 barrels -> 10/6; drink piles and a barrel-less pile untouched; still 10/6 after 200 days without cancels; follows a barrel leaving to 9; restored after 31 days without brewable plants; switch off). Older timed-freeze tests set `cancel.stand=false` (test_bug834_838, test_neu4_cancel).

## Live-Erwartung
i2 (and i1/i3/i4 once DF cancels for containers): the food piles that accept NO drink stop taking more barrels than they hold (floor 2), so a new batch of barrels is not eaten by plant hauling. Piles that accept any drink (pure, mixed with food, or `dfllm-drink`) are never capped. i2 and i1 are explicitly NOT promised to brew: the 130-day freeze of the same mechanism changed nothing measurable on i2, and the ~35 empties DF takes none of are unexplained (BUG-887 round 3 measures them). What the cap can show: the count of barrels with plants in them stops growing while barrels are made (`why items:barrels`), and the piles' limits in kern.log ('container cure: pile barrel limit written on N piles'). The cap is judged confirmed only if, after a barrel batch, the free-empty count in the BUG-887 snapshot stays up for 30 game days.

## Round 2 (verifier: no real exit, the cap only ratchets down, mixed and barrel-less piles leak)
- Exit: `M.pile_standing` also ends the cap when DF's container reason has been gone (gate closed: fewer than cx_min_n cancels in the window and, through the BUG-867 hysteresis, 14 further days under cx_off_n) for `economy.cancel.stand_calm_d` (30) days: `M.stand_end` puts the old limits back. When the cancels return, the gate opens and the cap starts again (a loop of about 45 + 30 days at worst is the price of not capping for good; the barrels the piles hold then stay).
- Floor: `act.pile_barrels('freeze', {floor = 2, room = 3})`: every food pile gets target = max(barrels it holds, 2); a pile whose barrels all left is capped at 2 (food keeps a container, but 56 piles cannot swallow 100+ barrels), a pile whose own limit is already under the target is never raised. Config `stand_floor` (0..50).
- Leak: food+drink piles are capped at barrels + `stand_mixed_room` (3) (drink barrels come and go; the drink emergency still caps them at what they hold); barrel-less piles at the floor 2. Measured on i2 (kern.log tick 210736512): all 56 capped piles and the 6 'left alone' ones are `dfllm-stock` kernel piles; the 6 left alone had limit 1 (0-1 barrels); the log does not list mixed piles (they are filtered out before it), so their count on i2 is unknown, the room of 3 bounds what each can swallow.
- Spoilage bound: a capped pile still takes 2 barrels at least and the plants it cannot put in barrels go to its bins/pots/floor as before; no spoilage measurement exists. If food rots, set `economy.cancel.stand_floor` higher or `stand=false`.
- Open (not fixed): C2 the ~30 empties DF skips; C4 the wooden barrel orders on i2 (order 6818 and the 10/8-batches): gamelog has no 'Make wooden barrel' attempt and no cancel since the last batch, the manager had 0 orders unvalidated at the end (cli.log y523), the mood reserve holds logs 15 of 20: the cause is not found, so the cure stays the direct Carpenter tasks of BUG-835; C6 Kiln clay pots are not built.
- Test: `tests/lua/test_bug887_891_fass.lua` (floor of 2 when all barrels leave, limit under the floor not raised, mixed pile at barrels+3, lifted after calm and capped again).

## Round 3 (verifier: the mixed-pile room was a one-time ratchet)
- Every stockpile that accepts DRINK (any `drink_plant` / `drink_animal` entry set: pure or mixed with food) is left completely uncapped in standing mode, also in the drink emergency; only piles that accept no drink are capped (target = max(barrels in the pile, floor 2), never raised). `stand_mixed_room` / `STAND_MIXED_ROOM` and the `room` argument are gone. Without a floor (the older timed freeze of BUG-834/844) the old rule stays: drink piles only when `mixed`.
- Test: `tests/lua/test_bug887_891_fass.lua`: the mixed pile (4 barrels, limit 40) and a pure drink pile stay at 40 on day 1; on day 2 the mixed pile holds 9 barrels and on day 3 one more, and the limit is still 40 (also with `mixed = true`), the pure food piles stay at what they hold.
## Round 4 (13c: the cap must not protect registered empties)
- Live (i2 25.11.): a stockpile keeps empty barrels as ITS containers (`storage.container_item_id`, see BUG-887 round 6); the cap that followed the barrels lying in the pile therefore protected exactly the empties no Still gets. `act.pile_barrels('freeze')` now leaves the registered EMPTY barrels out of the count (target = max(barrels lying there without registered empties, floor)); the standing cap's exit and floor are unchanged. `act.pile_barrels('release')` (BUG-887 round 6 D) lowers the piles that hold registered empties first (`brew_watch` order: release, then the standing cap); its old limits are kept in `p.pilecap` and come back through the same exit.
- Test: `tests/lua/test_bug887_round6_registry.lua` ('BUG-888 round 4 (E)'): a pile with 2 registered empties + 2 full barrels is capped at 2 (not 4), a pile whose barrels are all registered empties at the floor 2. The older BUG-888 tests are unchanged and stay valid (their piles register nothing); `test_bug887_891_fass` only filters the act list to `freeze` calls.
- Live-Erwartung: see BUG-887 round 6 (the standing cap shows `limit -> 2` for piles whose barrels were mostly registered empties; `container cure: pile barrel limit written on N piles` names them).
