# BUG-905 The quiet PEACE raise of B1 never comes: every unit within 20 tiles, soldiers at their station included, restarts its quiet window
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested); second round (15b): the round-15 exemptions are WITHDRAWN, the bridge says why it stays down
Severity: high
Found: Run 17 i3 Orangering (kernel b8160ba3, 25.11.2026), B1 85-87,64,142: ~35 `B1 up waits in PEACE: citizen/soldier 3-5 tiles from it, no walker near it for 645..2055 ticks needed` lines in game year 469, 0 raises; the return window lowers B1 for 600 ticks and it is never raised again, so B1 is DOWN when the alarms come (BUG-906..909). Round 15 (kernel 7f3706e5, y473-y474): `peace_wait need=661..1610` the whole time, the bridge stood down 130000-197000 ticks, 54 `peace_wait` events in 174,700 ticks (47 at 3-4 tiles, 5 at 5, 1 at 6, 1 at 7; 25 distinct units, 48 citizens, 6 soldiers), `peace_stuck` #38083 / #38414: never a gap of 620 ticks (let alone 1610) without a walker within 8 tiles. Diagnosis `leitung/neu10/DIAGNOSE-B1-2026-11-25.md` and the round-15 diagnosis (section C).
Module: `lua/dfllm/gate.lua` (`M.peace_blockers`, `M.peace_busy`, new `M.peace_traffic`, `M.peace_day`, `M.peace_wait`)

## Cause
`gate.lua` `M.peace_busy` (BUG-831) counted every living unit within `gate.peace_clear_r` (20) tiles of the footprint, standing or walking, soldiers at their station included. The squad station of i3 lies 4 tiles inside B1, so some soldier is always within 20 tiles, and one citizen on any path within 20 tiles restarts the window of max(600, measured lever latency 645..2055) ticks as well. The comment at the `PEACE_R` constant and `config/reflex.json` promised 'but a soldier at his station'; the code never did it.

## What round 15 did and why it is withdrawn
Round 15 (F1) exempted a soldier standing at his station at least 3 tiles out (`PEACE_STAND_D`) and a unit farther than 8 tiles (`PEACE_FAR`) whose path does not enter the footprint within 20 steps. The live result (kernel 7f3706e5) was NO raise in peace at all (the 3-6 tile ring is the corridor to the squad station and the levers 90,58 / 90,59; citizens use it constantly), and the same wait logic in the ALERT bounded wait (BUG-907) killed citizen 29043. A walker covers a tile per 9 ticks, the measured pull-to-moving was 117 (#38047), 760 (#38202) and ~400 (#38331 to #38337) ticks, moving to up another 100 to 380: 8 tiles are 72 ticks, so neither exemption is safe. The 'PEACE raise' of event #38339 was no PEACE raise (it was a RECOVERY raise at stage 2 of the bounded wait; see BUG-907). Withdrawn: `peace_free` is gone, `peace_blockers` lists every unit within `peace_clear_r`, a soldier at his station included (the BUG-831 rule as before round 15).

## Fix (what stays and is new)
- `M.peace_blockers` returns all units within `peace_clear_r` (20) except the job's worker and the bridge's pullers, nearest first; `M.peace_busy` the first one; `peace_wait` and the pending-pull cancel use it unchanged (BUG-831).
- `M.peace_traffic(K, name, r, sc, now, list)`: while a bridge is wanted up in quiet PEACE every pass counts who came within `peace_clear_r` (a unit not seen for 300 ticks counts again), who walked onto the footprint (a path onto it, `M.crossers`) and the longest stretch without anybody within 20 tiles (`r.pt`, reset with the want).
- `M.peace_day` (BUG-908's report, kept and extended): once per want, after a game day (`PEACE_DAY` 1200 ticks) GATE `peace_stuck` names the blockers by kind and distance AND DECISION_NEEDED `gate_peace_traffic` (A; `bridge`, `n` units, `crossings`, `ticks`, `gap`, `need`, `lat`, opts `reroute`, `relocate`, `lever`) says: `B1 stays down: 5 units within 20 tiles in 1215 ticks (4.1 per 1000), 5 onto it (4.1 per 1000), longest quiet gap 27 of 1000 needed (lever latency 1000): reroute, move station/levers or pull by hand`. That is a layout problem the kernel cannot raise its way out of: the player must see it. The question is wake-quiet (`config/wake.json`, `dfpilot/session.py` QUIET_DECISIONS).

## Cost
The inner bridge stays DOWN in peace on a busy fort (every fort whose traffic runs within 20 tiles of it): that is the promise's limit, not a defect; the alarm then meets a down bridge (BUG-907: the kernel raises it in ALERT only when nobody can reach it; SIEGE/BREACH keep the timed force and the bounded crush of rounds 3 and 12; the player's `lever` up of an inner bridge works in ALERT and SIEGE since 15b). Nobody is locked out by this rule; nobody is crushed by a PEACE raise that is never made.
