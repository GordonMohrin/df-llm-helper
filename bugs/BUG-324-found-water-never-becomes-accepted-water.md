# BUG-324 water the scan found never becomes accepted water: no hospital, no GiveWater source
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: 2026-10-29, Run 15 round 3 (Inkintense y376-377, Earthboat y384-385, live)
Module: `lua/dfllm/water.lua` (M.zone_found, M.zone_cands, M.on_attack_path, finish, give_diag, dry_well, inspect), `lua/dfllm/act.lua` (water_zone), contract/schema (act `water_zone`), `dfpilot/fairplay.py` (water keys), `dfpilot/bp/sites.py` (_near_water text)

## Actual
Inkintense: the water scan finished (kern.log `water sources: 675 tiles >= 4/7 in 12 bodies, 3 with a bank or a dig, 11 with a well spot`), state `water.wsc` = `water 88,102,132 (7/7): a well at 88,102,133 over it (open above, reachable)`, but `water.src` stayed `[]`, `hosp_ok 0`, `bp sites hospital` refused every site (`no accepted water in the fort`); no hospital for 4 years. Earthboat: the river (836 tiles of 7/7 water on z139 under its ramp tops on z140), no hospital zone (removed by BUG-034 at y382), a dry well 17700, patients at thirst 37,348 and `DECISION_NEEDED patient_thirst`: `1 GiveWater job open, none taken by 53 idle ... no accepted water at all`.

Root cause: `water.lua` (1d17c7e) only ever *described* the found water. The accepted sources are wells over water and Water Source zones over deep water (`phase_sites`, `zone_wet`); the scan's best way (`M.best_way`, `recipe` water.lua:656 'zone the water ... as Water Source') went into a recipe and `water.wsc` only, and no code zoned it (no act for a Water Source zone existed: contract.lua C.ACT). With `wet == 0` (water.lua:2176) the hospital gate and bp sites stayed closed for good. GiveWater: the game takes the water from a well or a Water Source zone; `give_diag` (water.lua:1584) then blamed the idle carers ('open, none taken by 53 idle'), the wrong lead.

## Fix
While no water is accepted, `M.zone_found` paints a 1x1 Water Source zone `dfllm-water` (new act `water_zone`, quickfort zone key `w`, owner water) on the scan's best water: a body with a reachable bank on its own level (the water tile beside the bank, `b.wt`), else a well spot (the water under the open tile, zoned on both levels: the carrier's floor is one level up, as fishing from above, BUG-296). Safe only: never cavern water, never with the bank or the water on the attack path (manifest Z1/Z2, killboxes), never in BREACH, outside PEACE/RECOVERY only a bank known roofed (`snapshot.outside_near`); roofed first, then the nearest; one zone every `wz_every` (2 days), at most `wz_max` (4) spots within `wz_forget` (28 days), a tried spot or one already inside a zone not again. The next water run reads the zone over deep water as accepted, `water.src`/`src_l` carry it (`zone N in Kern+`), and `bp sites hospital` ranks by it. The cause list names it (`Water Source zone dfllm-water painted at x,y,z (...): accepted after the next water run`), `inspect water_sources` has `wz`. `give_diag`: with no accepted water and no GiveWater job running the verdict is `nowater` ('no accepted water (no well over water, no Water Source zone): 1 GiveWater job open without a source'). `dry_well` names a well spot's floor as fill bank too. Config `water.wz`, `wz_every`, `wz_max`, `wz_forget` (fairplay ranges).
