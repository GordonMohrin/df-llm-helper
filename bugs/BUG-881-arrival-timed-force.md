# BUG-881 A raise waits for the recall stream until the hostile is 30 tiles out; its pull is cancelled for walkers 8-25 ticks after it is queued
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: Run 17d i4 Girderroots y418 spring d39 (events #13415-#13639): ARRIVAL snatcher (popup), two cave dragons 92 tiles from O1, both bridges down, 22 citizens outside; 19 pulls and 18 cancels in the first 905 ticks, BREACH at T+900, 18 citizens dead (pop 50 -> 32).
Module: `lua/dfllm/gate.lua` (`holders`, `control`, `M.pull_late`, `closing_fast`, `track`, `can_reach`)

## Root cause
`holders()`: in SIEGE/BREACH the raise is forced (only units ON the footprint or on its path's first step hold it) only with a hostile within force_near 3, or within approach_near 30 and CLOSING after the raise was held approach_hold 300 ticks. Before that every walker within CROSS_REACH 13 steps holds it, and the recall stream of 22 citizens always has one there: no pull for 401 ticks, then pulls cancelled 8-25 ticks after queueing. The hostile needs ~7 ticks a tile (92 tiles = ~650 ticks), the pull and the move ~200-300 ticks: the first force came at +452, too late. A forced pull was cancelled again 9 ticks later for a unit on the footprint although its worker was still on his way.

## Fix
- Timed force: with the nearest visible hostile closing in (2 tiles in 60 ticks, `closing_tpt`) and its trip `d * measured ticks-a-tile` (8..30, the i4 dragons 12.7, not RACE_FOE_TPT 8) <= `CLOSE_BUDGET` (1500) the raise is forced at once (no approach_hold). Building destroyers alone (`M.barrier_foe`: no census list entry without bd = 1) get no timed force. Round 3 checked the premise 'a raised bridge cannot be destroyed': the code and the logs say the opposite (BUG-462: trolls broke O1 and B2 while UP; BUG-030: `BRIDGE_DESTROYED ... last up`; the loss history of Run 11 site98: cave dragons broke O1 in all five sieges), so a raise does not stop them and the exclusion stays; the plain approach rules apply.
- Wait for a gap, bounded: while the trip is longer than `CLOSE_NEED` (400 ticks: worker walk + pull + move; y413 live: taken 58 ticks after queueing, up ~200 later) the timed force still holds the pull for units ON or beside the footprint and for walkers who can be on it before the bridge has stopped moving, by the speed each was MEASURED at (`track`/`can_reach`; unknown = 9). From `CLOSE_NEED` ticks before the hostile arrives (`rr.fz_c`) only the last `LATE_R` tiles of the worker's walk cancel the pull whoever stands on the footprint: a gapless stream (a walker within 4 steps all the time, 87 ticks apart) can no longer keep it pending for ever (review round 2: the first version held for ever, O1 up at +1359 and BREACH at +927 in that stream).
- Commit: a forced pull is not cancelled for units on the footprint while its worker is far from the lever (`M.pull_late`).
- The plain approach rule (BUG-587/775) is unchanged.
- Round 3, bounded crush: in the `fz_c` phase a unit ON the footprint keeps its hold until the hostile's eta is at most the measured lever latency (`st.lat.t`, else `CLOSE_NEED`) + `TIMED_WIN` 110 + `CRUSH_MARGIN` 60 (`gate.crush_late`, the last moment a raise can still succeed); only then the pull goes ahead and GATE_CRUSH_RISK (unit, `eta`, `forced` 1) is emitted once per pull (`gate.crush_forced`). PEACE rules (BUG-831..833) are untouched (a timed force exists only in SIEGE/BREACH).

## Lock-out and crush (decided, accepted)
A bridge that moves takes ~100 ticks and a citizen crosses a tile in ~27: a stream with a walker every 87 ticks has no gap that long. So once the wait has run (the army is CLOSE_NEED ticks away) the raise goes ahead: citizens still outside are locked OUT (alive; the doors do not wait for them, as BUG-775) and a citizen on the footprint at the moment it moves can be crushed (test: one or two). The alternative is no raise before the army is at the gate. No new mechanism for the locked-out: they stay in the recall, the seal/refuge rules of the fort apply (REACH / SEAL events). Against building destroyers alone nothing is locked out by the clock.
