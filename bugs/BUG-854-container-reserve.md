# BUG-854 The container cure was a one-shot batch: every batch of new barrels lifted drink for about one game year, then the stall came back; where wood is 0 usable it delivered nothing
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: Run 17 round 4 (main ac8bed9a), 24.-25.11.2026, `leitung/neu3/LIVE-DIAGNOSE-2026-11-24.md` Nachtrag 25.11. 02:30. i1 Diedplank: each batch of barrel orders lifts drink for about a year (14 d -> 108 d), then the stall is back. i4 Girderroots: `wood 0 usable of 361`, drink 0 d until the player sent `order.add MakeBarrel mat=rock x10..20` (drink up to 304 d, then down again). i2 Whirledrampart: the kernel cure needed 3 cycles / 2.7 game years (kern.log 'pile barrel limit written on 34 / 45 piles', '8 barrels at once' at ticks 197971200, 198245607, 198461491).
Module: `lua/dfllm/economy.lua` (`M.plan_reserve`, `M.reserve_mat`, `M.reserve_labor`, `M.rv`, `M.plan_pots`, `plan_containers`), `config/baseline.json` + `economy.DEFAULTS` `creserve`, `dfpilot/fairplay.py` (`ECONOMY_LIM`)

## Root cause
1. One shot: `plan_contx` ordered `min(15, max(8, brews))` barrels once per stall verdict (`p.contx`, renewed only while `brew_watch` judged 'nobarrel'); the plant piles' barrel limit was frozen for 14 days and not renewed for 14 more (`cx_pile_days`, `pilex`). The new barrels were taken by the piles' haulers (i2: barrels 321 -> 329 -> 346 while `barrels_empty` stayed 7) or filled with the drink they made, and nothing ordered the next batch before the next stall was judged.
2. i2's first two cycles: `economy task plan: ...economy.lua:1929: attempt to call a nil value (global 'stone_free')` (kern.log of Whirledrampart, three times, right after '8 barrels at once'): `M.plan_pots` called `stone_free`, a `local function` defined about 1,000 lines later, so it was a nil global. The call sits on the path of every cycle where `plan_contx` reaches the rock-pot relief, and it aborted the whole `T.plan` run (leather, cloth, gear, bins ... of that run were never planned). The relief the NEU4 doc promised ('rock pots') never ran, and it would have been useless: i4's gamelog holds 971 x 'Make rock pot' (= MakeTool ITEM_TOOL_LARGE_POT, the kernel's `plan_pots`) without a lift of drink, while the players' `mat=rock` command shows up as 'Make rock barrel (10)', '(15)', '(20)' = exactly their amounts: a real BARREL item, not a large pot.
3. Wood: with `wood 0 usable` (BUG-855: 336 of 365 logs built into walls) the wooden batch has nothing to make barrels of (i4, i1).

## Fix
`M.plan_reserve` (called first by `plan_containers` for barrels; the old `plan_contx` stays the fallback for a stall without DF's reason):
- Control on DF's own signal (`M.cx_reason`: at least `cancel.cx_min_n` 'Needs empty food storage item' cancels in the 7-day window) and brew orders open (`r.s.brew.left > 0`), never on the census count.
- Batch = `ceil(brews open / brews_per)` clamped to `min_batch`..`max_batch` (5 / 10 / 30). A new batch only after the last one ended (its order closed: `rs.done`) AND DF cancelled again AFTER it (a cancel newer than `done + grace_days`), at least `repeat_days` apart; after `max_repeats` such batches in a row only every 14 days. So it repeats until the cancels stop and not longer. While DF still cancels and the last batch has had no effect yet, nothing else (the old x15 batch, pots) orders.
- Cancels stopped: a trickle of `trickle_n` (1) container every `trickle_days` (20) days while brews are open and `cont_free < trickle_free` (20); started only after the cure ran once (`p.rsv.t0`), never in a fort DF never cancelled. Why this proxy: the live stalls consumed 15-20 containers a game year (each brew job fills one container), the census cannot count the containers DF takes (stale `container` flag, phantoms), and a count of 'barrels created after the cure that DF took at least once' needs per-item history the kernel does not keep. Everything is bounded by `total_cap` (200 containers in all; one warn line a week when reached).
- Material: rock (`{job = MakeBarrel, material = INORGANIC}`, the exact spec `order.add mat=rock` builds through `act.workorder`, `uses = {rock = 1}`, Craftsdwarf's workshop, Stone crafting labor) when the free boulders are at least batch + `rock_plenty` (20) or usable wood is below the batch; else wood (Carpenters, Carpentry). Neither: no order, one warn line a game day, the old planning goes on.
- Labor: once a game day `cx_labor` tops the holders of the material's labor up to `max(3, min(6, 2 + brews / 10))` (idle first, best skill, never soldiers); the manager-less `cx_direct` MakeBarrel tasks stay for wood while a container stall is told.
- `M.plan_pots`: the forward reference goes through `M.stone_free` (set after the definition).
- kern.log: `container reserve: N rock barrels ordered (DF cancelled brew Nx in 7 d; B brews open; U usable logs, R free boulders; M ordered in all)`, `container reserve: the batch of N rock barrels is made (M ordered in all)`, `container reserve: N containers wanted (...) and nothing to make them of`, `container reserve: total_cap ... reached`.

## Config
`economy.creserve` = {on true, min_batch 10, max_batch 30, brews_per 5, grace_days 2, repeat_days 3, max_repeats 4, trickle_days 20, trickle_n 1, trickle_free 20, rock_plenty 20, total_cap 200}; `lint --config` checks ranges and unknown keys. The switch `economy.diag.cx_cure` gates it as before. (The name `creserve` is not `economy.reserve`, which holds the moods' stock floors.)
