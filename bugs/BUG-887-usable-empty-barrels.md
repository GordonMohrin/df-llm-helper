# BUG-887 Empty barrels counted as free that no brew can take
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: Run 17 i1 / i2 (25.11.): census 'empty' barrels 27-42 beside DF's 'Needs empty food storage item' 100-250x a week for a year (i2 y500-y522: 29-42 empties that never change); the `why items:barrels --raw` samples show trader barrels (refs UNIT_TRADEBRINGER without the trader flag) and barrels lying in the mood pile `dfllm-mood` (max_barrels=1).
Module: `lua/dfllm/economy.lua` (`classify` BARREL branch, `M.barrel_trader_ref`, `M.mood_pile_at`, `M.cont_words`, `M.cont_text`)

## Root cause
CONFIRMED on i2 (diagnosis C2, partly): the census already skipped barrels with the item flags trader / in_building / forbid / dump (the skip mask), a barrel held by a job and a barrel on a tile with no walk group; it did NOT skip a barrel whose owner is a merchant (general ref UNIT_TRADEBRINGER) nor a barrel in the one-barrel mood pile. Both were `barrels_empty` and `cont_free` although no Still job can take them. The wording everywhere said 'N empty barrels' (stall line, ladder, DECISION text), so a fort with 42 'empty' and 0 usable read as 'has barrels'. NOT found: why DF skips the remaining ~30 'free' empties of i2 (unknown 1 of the diagnosis; the stale `container` flag idea stays a hypothesis).

## Fix
Census: each empty barrel is counted ONCE under the first reason that holds: `barrels_trader` (ref UNIT_TRADEBRINGER), `barrels_mood` (inside a `dfllm-mood` pile rectangle), `barrels_busy` (a job holds it), `barrels_far` (no walk group), else `cont_free` (usable). `barrels_forb` counts forbidden barrels (never in `barrels`). `M.cont_words` / `M.cont_text` give '7 usable of 42 empty barrels or pots (20 a trader's, 10 in a mood pile, 5 held by a job, 3 forbidden (not counted))'; used by the brew stall line, the 'nobarrel' verdict (`not usable: ...`), the ladder ('no usable empty barrel or pot: ...'), the DECISION text and state `stock.contw` (brief: `containers 7 usable of 42 empty (...)`). The reserve, the ladder and the stall verdict already read `cont_free`, so they now see the smaller number.

## Live-Erwartung
- After a reload, i1-i4 write, within one game day of the first container cancel DF prints, a kern.log line 'brew cancel snapshot: ...' and a `BREW_CANCEL_SNAPSHOT` event, then at most one per day while the cancels go on. Each carries the flag census of the empties (free and all: container set/clear, flag-combination and general_ref histograms, from the one existing barrel pass) and the flags/refs of the barrel DF attached to the brew job's container slot.
- i1 (Diedplank) and i2 (Whirledrampart) are explicitly NOT promised to brew: this round changes no behaviour of the brew chain apart from counting mood-pile barrels as usable again (the status line may show a larger 'usable' number than before). The point is the measurement.
- The cause counts as CONFIRMED only if the snapshot of a fort that cancels (>= 5 per 7 days) shows one of these, with ids from `why brew --raw` / the event: (a) `free` > 0 but `job`/`haul` explains the gap (a hauling job holds the empties DF would take) -> reservation race; (b) `free` > 0 with `out` == `free` or `near` far above the brewers' reach -> surface/distance; (c) H1 (phantom empties through an item flag, decided by the snapshot ALONE): `free` > 0, none of (a)/(b), and the flag census of the same snapshot (`fcs`/`fcc` = free empties with the `container` flag set / clear, `combo` = histogram of all census flags of the free empties, `refs` = their general_ref types) shows the free empties carrying a flag or ref (`container`, `foreign`, `in_inventory`, `artifact`, `spider_web`, `dead_dwarf`, UNIT_TRADEBRINGER, ENTITY_ITEMOWNER ...) that the barrel(s) listed under `taken` (id, ALL flags, refs of the barrel DF attached to the brew job's container job_item, job_item_idx 1) do NOT carry; the discriminator is then that flag/ref, and the confirming ids are the `taken` barrel and the free ones in `combo`. If `taken` shows the same flags as the free empties, H1 is refuted (DF takes a barrel of the same kind: reservation race or per-attempt cause). `taken` is empty when no brew job holds a barrel at that moment: then read the next day's snapshot; `combo_all`/`refs_all`/`acs`/`acc` give the same census over all empties for comparison; (d) `cancels` huge with `done` > 0 and `stills` large -> the Still count multiplies the cancels (not a missing container: completions go on). If `free` == 0 the census was wrong; if `free` > 0 and none of (a)-(d) fits, the cause stays unknown and the next step is a wider flag list in `brewsnap.CENSUS_FLAGS`.
- NOT promised: that any barrel counted usable is taken by DF.
