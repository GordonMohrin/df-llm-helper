# BUG-830 the first melee squad's weapon spec flipped between a real and a wooden training weapon: empty hands, the growth gate never saw all members armed
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: 2026-11-24, Run 17 i3 Orangering y375 (pop 29/30): `ready.gh.need` "first squad: 1 of 2 members hold a real weapon (need 3+, all armed)", `mil.free weapon 12`, census melee weapons 5-15, `mil.kit {n 6, weapon 2, wood 2, tr 2}`; i3 commands.log `act.squad_uniform` on squad A position 0 alternates real (HAMMER_WAR 153731639, AXE_BATTLE 153898710 / 153970738) and training (SPEAR_TRAINING 153818039, AXE_TRAINING 153927511) every 29000-86000 ticks, the real ones each with kern.log "squad A: 1 positions keep a real weapon: the fort holds no free training weapon for them ... a soldier with empty hands is no soldier" (153731639, 153898710; 2 positions at 153970738)
Module: `lua/dfllm/military.lua` (`ensure_weapons`, new `M.first_real`, `M.kit_rows`, new `M.trace_first`), `lua/dfllm/kit.lua` (`KIT.soldier` field `hand`)

## Actual
The training policy (FR-89/BUG-598) puts every melee position on a wooden training weapon while the pool holds one; the pool is minus the wooden specs of the other squads and the fort has one or two (the carpenters make them: `order dfllm:train_axe x1` only at 153899362). Squad A (the one the ladder reads) and B share it: a position gets the wooden spec when the pool shows one and goes back to a real weapon when it shows none. Each change of the spec makes the soldier drop what he holds and walk off for the other weapon, and the ladder needs ALL members armed at its look. The weapons were in stock (census g_weapon 5-15, mil.free 12), so on i3 the gate was held by this churn plus the seat ceiling (BUG-827), not by a missing weapon. CONFIRMED from the i3 commands.log/kern.log lines above (the flips). NOT confirmed: that the one 'not armed' member of the 17:31 state had empty hands from a flip at that very moment: per-soldier equipment was not logged (this fix adds it).
i2 (A has 3 seats, kit wood 0, tr 0, g_weapon 51): its members are NOT on the training policy, so this flip is not its cause; why 1-2 of A's three hold no weapon there is not proven (candidates in kern.log: body-spec refit toggling metal/any every ~3 days, "gear pieces DF kept assigned ... unfetched for 2400 ticks" released again and again, soldiers called back). The per-soldier trace below is there to settle it. i2's hold at 30 is meanwhile the drink step (BUG-829 part 2).

## Fix
1. `M.first_real(K, key)`: the first melee squad is not put on the wooden policy while the growth ladder is on and the census holds real melee weapons for all its members (`g_weapon >= max(growth.armed_min, members)`): `ensure_weapons` then treats it as in an alert (real weapons via `weapon_plan`), in PEACE/DRILL too. One full first squad takes real free weapons at once; the training policy stays for every other squad and for this one when the fort has no real melee weapons. The gate condition is not changed (fail closed: no real weapons, no armed squad). Z5 (drill stays wooden): `first_real` ends once the ladder's 'squad' step has been passed once: `kit_stats` sets the latch `rt.fr_pass = <squad key>` (kern.log "first squad A fully armed (n of n, k backed by a waiting weapon): back on the training policy"); from then on squad A is on the training policy like every other squad, and a wooden weapon in hand counts as armed while a real one waits for the soldier (the existing `back_rows` rule, wb). So A holds real weapons only while the step is open (days), never for the life of the fort. The latch is in `rt` (a reload re-derives it from the next `kit_stats`); no hysteresis beyond it, the ladder's own `drop_days` damps a later step loss.
2. `M.trace_first` (called from `kit_stats`, once per game day or when the picture changes, only while the first squad is not fully armed): one kern.log line "first squad A 1 of 3 armed (census melee weapons 15): pos 0 hand wood:AXE_TRAINING spec ... (backed); pos 1 hand none spec AXE_BATTLE (not armed)": per soldier the weapon in hand (real/wood/none and which), the weapon spec of the position, backed by a waiting real weapon or not. spec with hand none = unfetched, spec none = empty position.
