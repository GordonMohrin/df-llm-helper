# BUG-317 a fort entered over its drawbridges only gets no trap lane: "0 door(s)", traps 0, no traps draft
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: medium
Found: 2026-10-29, Run 15 Earthboat (runs/run-15b-earthboat/FR-BUGS.md 'Runde 3', y383 spr - y385 spr, pop 83, a siege of 35 goblins)
Module: `dfpilot/bp/traps.py` (bridge_ways, parse_at/format_at/pieces), `dfpilot/traps.py` (expand, bridge_lanes, covered, facts, draft, gate), `dfpilot/plan.py` (propose)

## Actual
Earthboat is entered only over its drawbridges O1 (outer) and B1 (inner); `dfllm bp sites traps` said `no way in left to trap (0 door(s) between the outside and the fort)`, `traps 0`, no `traps N/M`, and `plan propose` never drafted a traps build in P5 (G14 red). Offline on a copy of the live save folder (snapshot c1a188ba925ebbf901): `bp.traps.ways_in` returns no way, `traps.facts` `{doors 0, open 0}`.

Root cause: `dfpilot/bp/traps.py:247` (1d17c7e) `ways_in` looks at door tiles (`+`) only, so a way in over a bridge is never a way; `dfpilot/traps.py:148` `draft` and `:159` `gate` read `fort.traps.open` (the door lanes) only, so a fort without a door between the outside and the fort gets neither a draft nor a warning. FR-96's own limits named it ("a bridge as a side entrance gets no lane; O1 is the main path: the hall and the killing field cover it"), but the hall's traps are fortcore stage 4 (P6), so a P5 fort with bridges only had none.

## Fix
`bp.traps.bridge_ways`: one lane per outer or inner manifest bridge whose footprint stands on the snapshot (`=`/`H`, not `replaced`). Walking out from the footprint on its level (doors, hatches and bridges closed) through the tiles that neither the outside nor the fort's interior reaches (Z1 bailey, Z2 hall, killboxes and the passages between them), it takes the nearest 8 tiles (`BRIDGE_LANE`) of the hall, a killbox or a passage, at most 16 tiles (`OFF_BRIDGE`) from the footprint centre: for O1 the hall's first tiles past the bailey exit, for B1 the killing field K1 in front of it. Never on or beside a bridge footprint (the gate's ring), never in Z1 or beside it (depot and wagon route; the bailey exit stays free as stage 4 keeps it), never in Z3/Z4, a room, a station, a stair, the refuge or on an edge tile, never in the outside (the approach in front of O1 is the wagon road). A tile in two lanes belongs to the nearer bridge. A `^`, a tile of the hall's entry Z2 (at=hall restocks it), of a traps project under way or of a placed fortcore stage 4 keeps its lane slot and is not wanted again (stage 4 placed later over a lane trap counts it as pre-occupied, runner.lua `pre_occupied`).
Template `traps` `at=bridges`: the CLI fills in `bx,y,z:dx,dy ...` (way = the footprint centre), kind stone only (DOCTRINE: no cage trap on the main path; `topo.audit` fails a cage trap in Z1/Z2/K1), the manifest entry `T<x>_<y>_<z>` with `way`, so `lua/dfllm/traps.lua` counts it as a way (`ways w/wn`). `at=ways` with nothing left names the bridge ways: `...; 2 bridge way(s) want a lane: traps at=bridges`. `traps.facts` adds `bridges, bopen, bways`; plan propose drafts `{"tpl":"traps","site":"S1","p":{"at":"bridges"}}` from P5 once per year while a bridge way wants a lane (note `BUG-317: 2 bridge way(s) without a trap lane (O1, B1)`), beside `at=ways` for doors; plan check warns while the plan has no `at=bridges` build. Citizens: DF's traps do not fire on citizens or their animals, and the gates' raise/lower rules are unchanged.
