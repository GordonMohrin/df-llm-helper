# BUG-878 Container reserve kept a dead wooden MakeBarrel order for ever and never read DF's cancels of its own job
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: Run 17 i1 Diedplank 25.11.: kern.log 'container reserve: 10 rock barrels ordered (DF cancelled brew 140x ...; 0 usable logs, 18 free boulders; 59 ordered in all)' then 'order dfllm:barrelx x10', yet gamelog.txt shows 476x 'cancels Make wooden barrel: Needs logs' in its last 3000 cancel lines; drink 0 (runs/run-17a/FR-BUGS.md ~l.198). i4 (E:/DF-test4): the player's `order.add MakeBarrel mat=rock` and the reserve's own rock batches made real rock barrels.
Module: `lua/dfllm/economy.lua` (`M.plan_reserve`, `M.reserve_mat`, new `M.reserve_dead` / `M.reserve_out` / `M.reserve_cancels`, `M.dead_wood_barrel` in `needs_cancel`)

## Root cause
The rock order was NOT the defect: commands.log of i1 shows the reserve's rock batches as `{job MakeBarrel, material INORGANIC, tag dfllm:barrelx}`, byte for byte the player's `order.add MakeBarrel mat=rock` (`{job MakeBarrel, material INORGANIC, tag dfllm:cmd}`); DF names it 'Make rock barrel' (gamelog: 'Make rock barrel (10) has been completed' x5, '(1)' x4, 'masterpiece claystone barrel'). The wooden cancels come from WOODEN MakeBarrel orders: the reserve's own wood batch (commands.log tick 165258044: `item_conditions WOOD>=1, material_category [wood]`, ordered when the census said '10 usable logs' beside 253 logs on another walk group) and the ordinary `dfllm:barrel` x4 wooden orders. Nothing ever removed them: `economy.lua` `KEEP_JOBS` (needs_cancel, FR-10) keeps every MakeBarrel order however often DF cancels it, and `plan_reserve` returned `open(r, tag)` = true as long as the dead wooden `dfllm:barrelx` stood, so no rock batch followed and the reserve counted the wooden batch as 'ordered' (`rs.made`). The reserve never looked at the cancel tally of its own job ('make wooden barrel' / 'needs logs'), only at the brew cancels.

## Fix
`M.reserve_dead`: while a batch stands, the gamelog tally of its OWN job (wood: kind 'make wooden barrel' reason 'needs logs'; rock: 'make rock barrel' / 'make stone barrel' reason 'needs ...') is read since the batch was ordered; `creserve.dead_n` (10) cancels take the order back (`act.order_remove`), the unmade containers leave `made` (the cap is not burnt), `rs.out[mat]` marks the material out for `creserve.dead_days` (3) and the other material is ordered in the same run (no repeat gap, no grace). `M.reserve_out`: DF's words win over the census: dead_n cancels of a material's job within 2 game days keep it out of `reserve_mat` (also for a wooden batch the census would allow); when neither material is left the way-out line (once a week) quotes DF's words and nothing is called 'ordered'. `reserve_mat` subtracts the logs held by open orders too. `M.dead_wood_barrel`: a WOODEN MakeBarrel order DF cancels 'Needs logs' while the census holds no usable log is no longer a kept supply order (needs_cancel removes it after `orders.needs_drop` cancels, own tags get the usual backoff); a rock barrel order and a fort with logs keep the old rule.
