# BUG-860 The growth gate and the latch counted the first squad differently: the cap stayed 30 '(squad)' for good
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: Run 17 i3 Orangering 25.11. (`runs/run-17c/FR-BUGS.md` 'Messung Trupp A' and 'Teil 2'); same picture on i2 ('1 of 3' with 91 free weapons), i4 ('Trupp A 0 von 3' for 20 game years).
Module: `lua/dfllm/military.lua` (`M.kit_stats`, `M.sq_latch`, `M.squad_ready`, `M.assigned_real`, `M.item_block`, `M.trace_first`, `M.state`), `lua/dfllm/readiness.lua` (`M.growth_ladder`, `growth`), `lua/dfllm/util/contract.lua`, `dfpilot/schema.py`, `dfpilot/leiter.py`

## Symptom
kern.log of i3 (`E:/DF-test3/dfllm-runtime/region1-site97/kern.log`): at 1795575427 `military: first squad A fully armed (3 of 3, 2 backed by a waiting weapon): back on the training policy` (latch `rt.fr_pass`, military.lua kit_stats), and from then on every look `first squad A 1 of 3 armed (census melee weapons 8): pos 0 hand wood:SPEAR_TRAINING spec any (not armed); pos 1 hand real:SPEAR spec any; pos 2 hand wood:SPEAR_TRAINING spec SPEAR_TRAINING (not armed)`, and `readiness` said `first squad: 1 of 3 members hold a real weapon (need 3+, all armed)`. The halt `ceiling growth 30 (squad)` never left.

## Root cause
Two criteria for the same squad. The latch counted a wooden weapon in hand as armed while a real one waits (`back_rows`, only on the training policy: `tr` or `un`), the gate (`growth_ladder`: `sq_w >= sq_n`, all members) counted only what `kit_stats` saw after the squad went back to wooden drill. The doctrine (PLAYBOOK Z5) makes the squad drill with wooden weapons in PEACE, so the gate could not stay passed there. In i3 the player ran `squad.uniform kind=melee` at 1795575638 (commands.log) which sets `tr = 0` (military.lua `squad_uniform`: `e.tr, e.td = 0`), after which `back_rows` backed nobody ('not armed' with 7 spare weapons in the census), see BUG-861.

## Fix: ONE rule, `military.squad_ready`
The first melee squad is READY when it has >= `growth.armed_min` members that are FIT (`healthy`: no wound that needs treatment, awake, not starving) AND for each of them a real melee weapon in hand, or assigned to the position and usable (`M.assigned_real`: exists, no training weapon, not forbidden / melting / dumping / owned / in a building / carried by another / in a forbidden bin / on another walk group than the soldier: `M.item_block`), or waiting on the training policy (`back_rows`, unchanged). `kit_stats` counts `sq_n` members, `sq_f` fit, `sq_a` with a usable real weapon (fit or not), `sq_w` = fit AND armed = ready, `sq_u` unfit; a fourth member without a weapon no longer blocks (the old gate wanted ALL armed).
Latch (`M.sq_latch`, state `rt.sq`, `rt.fr_pass` for BUG-830's `first_real`): passes once `sq_w >= armed_min`; released when the squad has fewer members than `armed_min` (at once), or fewer than `armed_min` members with a usable real weapon (fit or not: a wound heals) for `M.SQ_RELEASE` = 6000 ticks (5 game days), or when the first squad is another one. An unreadable `armed_min` never passes (fail closed). A wooden drill weapon in PEACE does not un-pass it.
The same state feeds the ladder's `squad` step (`growth_ladder` takes `sq_ok`; without it `sq_w >= armed_min`), the halt text `first squad: 2 of 3 fit; a real weapon usable for 1 of 3 (need 3+ ready)`, `state mil.kit` (`sq`, `sqw` = ready, `sqf`, `sqa`, `sqok`), the kern.log trace (`first squad A 1 of 3 ready (...)`, per member `(assigned)` / `(backed)` / `(not fit)`) and the leiter hint (`dfpilot/leiter.py`: the Ruestung 'free weapons' hint is silent when `sqok = 1`, else names fit / usable counts).
