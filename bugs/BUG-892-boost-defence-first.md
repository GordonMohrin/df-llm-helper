# BUG-892 T.boost raised the oldest untaken jobs first: the seal's wall job stayed untaken for 600 game days beside 30 dig jobs
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high (the perimeter of i4 Girderroots stood open at 98,86,143 for over 600 game days; 'PERIMETER OPEN' in the status)
Found: Run 17d i4 Girderroots y437-y460 (25.11.2026, read-only diagnosis `leitung/neu10/DIAGNOSE-BAU-2026-11-25.md`): the seal's wall is DF job 1733178 (ConstructBuilding, flags=[], no worker, block 1530695 attached, not suspended, posting_index 105); nobody took it, the kernel never raised it. Three seal projects for the tile were set aside. `inspect workload` 'boost.raised' listed dig jobs only; the adversarial check showed T.boost does run (the raised list changes), so the wall waited in a queue of 'oldest first, 4 a day' behind 30 untaken dig jobs and older builds. (The first diagnosis' claim 'T.boost has not run for 170 days' is refuted.)
Module: `lua/dfllm/economy.lua` (`T.boost` ~l.14738, `M.boost_class`, `M.boost_tiles`, `M.dedicate`), `lua/dfllm/runner.lua` (`M.seal_tiles`)

## Cause
`T.boost` (BUG-747) raised (flag `do_now`, DFHack's `prioritize` cure) every untaken, unsuspended ConstructBuilding or designation-dig job after `BOOST.days` (3) days, oldest first, at most `BOOST.per_day` (4) a day and `BOOST.max` (8) raised-and-untaken at once. Nothing put a defence job ahead of 30 dig jobs: the wall job (newer than the ones in the queue) waited behind them, and each raised dig job that DF did not take blocked one of the 8 places.

## Fix
Defence first, in three classes (`M.boost_class`): (1) a ConstructBuilding job on a tile that a seal / plug project not done still has to build (`runner.seal_tiles`) or that is a way in of the entrance check (`seal.entrances`), with its items attached: raised AT ONCE (no 3 day wait), outside the per-day cap, with its own cap `BOOST.seal_max` 4 raised-and-untaken; (2) the other defence buildings (bridges, levers and traps, their gears: `M.DEF_KINDS`): the 3 days as before but before the rest; (3) the rest oldest first as before. What raising does: `job.flags.do_now = true` (only on a job that is not suspended, has no worker and, for classes 1/2, has its items attached; a job whose item list cannot be read counts as attached). `M.dedicate` takes a class-1 job without waiting its 3 more days. `inspect workload` lists the class-1 jobs first (the 8 shown), with `cl`. PEACE and RECOVERY only, as before (the siege code keeps its own order).

## Tests
`tests/lua/test_bug892_893_bau.lua`: the wall job raised on first sight beside 10 older dig jobs, the dig jobs after 3 days, 4 a day (the seal job did not use the day); a way in of the entrance check is class 1, a job without an item is not; the cap of 4 and the freed place; nothing in a SIEGE; class 2 before older plain builds. No bridge, gate, refuge or lockdown behaviour is touched (only a job flag in PEACE/RECOVERY), so no siege traffic test beyond the mode test.

## Live-Erwartung
Within 3 game days of the reload `inspect what=workload` shows `boost.raised` with job 1733178 (or its successor for 98,86,143) first (cl 1), `why job:<id> --raw` shows `flags=[do_now]`, kern.log has 'N seal / perimeter construction job raised to top priority at once', and the status line 'PERIMETER OPEN' disappears within 30 game days; or a new event says precisely why not (ORDER_STUCK kind worker: 'only N held BUILD_CONSTRUCTION' / SEAL_STUCK 'no free adult citizen has the labor Building construction ...'). NOT promised: that DF hands the job to a builder when every holder is busy with other work, nor material for a wall (stone, wood: residual, not in scope).
