# BUG-621 Siege start: "+41 more out since" counted citizens inside Kern+, and a forced raise of B1 was cancelled again once the hostile left the census
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high (3 citizens dead outside Tiefe+ at the siege start, BREACH `B1 not up at T+900`)
Found: 2026-11-09, Run 16d i4 Trumpetrealms y431 (SIEGE_START with a cave dragon, a goblin army and a snatcher within one day; ARRIVAL `recall: 5 of 7 still outside Tiefe+ after 10 t, +41 more out since`; GATE_FAIL `B1 not up after 900 ticks (blocked: citizen 26277 next to it, never raised onto them)`)
Module: `lua/dfllm/sense.lua` (`scan_citizens`), `lua/dfllm/reflex.lua` (`measure`, `recall_step`, `recall_text`), `lua/dfllm/gate.lua` (`holders`, `new_epoch`, `control`)

## Actual
- (a) kern.log of every siege of the run: `the civ alert moved from Kern+ to Tiefe+ 9 ticks after it sounded: 46 citizens not in Tiefe+ now`, then `20 more citizens out of Tiefe+ since the alert (9 ticks; not out at its start): 21775:job:Eat@85,97,136 ... 26278:down@73,79,136`, `+41 more out since`. Nobody walked out in 9 ticks: the 41 stood inside Kern+ (z 136-139, the hall) and had not walked down yet. The 7 who were really outside at the alert (26272 at 42,17,141 and 26787 at 148,33,141 on another walk group, 26941 and 29793 beside O1) were the ones that mattered; the number buried them and the note of the y431 player ("people keep walking out after the alert") followed from it.
- (b) y431 spr d60: `B1 raised with citizen 29657 next to it: a hostile 29 tiles from it and closing, held 300 ticks (approach_near 30)` (round 38 worked once), 350 ticks later `B1 up pull cancelled: citizen 26814 3 tiles from it and walking toward it` four times, `BREACH B1 not up at T+900`. y431 win: the same shape.

## Root cause
- (a) `sense.lua scan_citizens` counted every sane non-soldier citizen on no tile of the recall's burrows as `away`; after the alert moved from Kern+ to Tiefe+ (siege's deep defense, `REFUGE_SWITCH deeper`) that is all of Kern+. `reflex.lua measure` put every away id that was not in the start set into `new` and `recall_step` published `nnew = tot - still - extra`. Nothing in the numbers separated "inside the walls, on the way down" from "out".
- (b) `gate.lua holders` forces a SIEGE / BREACH raise (BUG-587) only in the frames in which a hostile is visible within `approach_near` AND closing AND the raise was held `approach_hold` ticks. The pull is queued in such a frame, but the lever worker takes it 85-455 ticks later (live), the hostile is out of the census or stopped (`closing` needs 4 tiles of progress between samples 150-900 ticks old) by then, the force is gone, and the pending-pull branch cancels the taken pull for any unit within 3 tiles that comes closer (`M.approach`, BUG-251/311): a pacing citizen cancels it every few frames.

## Fix
- (a) `sense.lua`: census `away_in` / `away_in_ids` = the away citizens that stand inside Kern+ or the refuge walls (sorted, <= 40). `reflex.lua measure`: those ids are no `new`; `m.tr` counts them (apart from the start set), `nnew` excludes them; ARRIVAL / RECALL text `recall: 5 of 7 still outside Tiefe+ after 10 t, 41 more inside on the way` (only people who really stepped out are `+N more out since`); the kern.log line of the alert switch says `(41 of them inside the walls, on the way in, no one is out)`. The alert itself was already immediate (arrival tick, same tick as the burrow restriction); the `civ alert: Kern+` -> `Tiefe+` switch is 9 ticks.
- (b) `gate.lua`: `M.APPROACH_STICKY` (900): the first frame that forces a raise by approach records `fz_until` / `fz_d` on the bridge runtime; for 900 ticks afterwards the raise stays forced whatever the hostile does (out of sight, halted), at the last distance. Only a unit ON the footprint still holds it (as before: BUG-310); the force ends with the want (bridge reached its state) and with a new epoch. A hostile that never qualified (parked army, BUG-492) forces nothing.
