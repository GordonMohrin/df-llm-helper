# BUG-543 A drill (and any non-emergency raise) pulled B1 with a soldier about to step onto it: citizen 26591 crushed
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: critical (a death in an exercise)
Found: 2026-11-06, Run 16c i3 Sealcertain y406 autumn d65 (event #6659; `runs/run-16c/FR-BUGS.md` "Tod 26591 zerquetscht von Bruecke B1 beim Drill"; `E:/DF-test3/dfllm-runtime/r16c4-sealcertain-site97/` kern.log + events.jsonl read only)
Module: `lua/dfllm/gate.lua` (`holders`, `M.raise_movers`, `M.drill_skip`, `M.occupied`), `lua/dfllm/drill.lua` (`measure`)

## Actual
DRILL at tick 163977019 (settled at 163977769: "soldiers on station 5 of 7"). events.jsonl: `pull B1 up` 163978076, `B1 moving` 163978328 (the lever was done 252 ticks later), `GATE_CRUSH_RISK soldier 26591 on the footprint at 63,116,140` 163978419, `BRIDGE_CRUSH` / DEATH 163978428, `B1 up`. kern.log has no "B1 up waits for ..." line between the pull and the move: gate saw nobody within the margin and nobody walking onto the footprint, from the pull to the lever, and the soldier stood on the footprint during the 100 ticks the bridge moved.

## Root cause
`gate.lua` `holders` (the one check in front of the only lever pull, `control` -> `K.act.pull`, and again every frame of a pending pull) held a raise for (a) units within `MARGIN` (1) of the footprint and (b) units whose read walk path (`unit.path.path`, `M.crossers`) enters it within `CROSS_REACH` (13) steps. A unit 2-3 tiles out with no readable path (a soldier marching between squad orders, shuffling about a station point, a path DF has not planned yet) is neither: `crossers` fails open on an unreadable path. He stepped onto the footprint after the lever was done, when nothing could cancel the pull any more. For DRILL (a TIMED mode) the deadline escape (`CROSS_HELD`) additionally released everything but units on the footprint or standing within `CROSS_ESCAPE`. The same code serves every non-emergency raise (PEACE / ALERT / RECOVERY gate raises, the inbox `lever` verb, the drill): there is no other lever pull in the code (`act.pull` is called at one place).

## Fix
`gate.raise_movers` (called from `holders` for every raise outside SIEGE / BREACH): a unit within `RAISE_R` (3) tiles of the footprint that has not stood on one tile for `RAISE_STILL` (60) ticks holds the raise, path or no path (a unit seen for the first time counts as moving; a tame animal and the bridge's own pullers in their room do not; a unit standing still does not, so a station does not stall the drill). The deadline escape (`CROSS_HELD`) exists only for SIEGE / BREACH. Where a raise finds no gap it waits (the held time is no deadline), ends as GATE_FAIL `blocked` naming the units ("soldier 26591 moving 2 tiles from it") after `DEADLINE` ticks of it, and the drill's T+900 drop (`gate.drill_skip`) records who kept the bridge down: `gate.occupied(K, name)`; `drill.measure` reports `occupied:B1` (not `up:B1`, `mech` 0) and kern.log says `drill: B1 not raised: occupied by ...`. SIEGE / BREACH keep the documented rules (BUG-310 on-the-footprint always holds, BUG-492 walkers, BUG-510 animals). A raise is still pulled while a unit stands 2 tiles away for good, so the drill does not stall on a station.
