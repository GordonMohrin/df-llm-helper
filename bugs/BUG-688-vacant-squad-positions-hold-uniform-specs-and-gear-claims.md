# BUG-688 Vacant squad positions hold uniform specs and DF reserves the free gear for them; no reason per empty slot
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: 2026-11-10, Run 16d i4 Trumpetrealms y447-y456 (FR-BUGS: 'squad.uniform B melee: 10 positions' and B stays at armor 0 / fit 0 beside 12-22 free body armors; worn 82 -> 29 % over the years, 0 of 11 soldiers fully equipped); Run 16a i1 Basementflew (worn swings 13-75 %, 5 of 12 fit)
Module: `lua/dfllm/military.lua` (`ensure_uniform`, `squad.uniform`, `M.vacant_sweep`, `M.slot_report`), `lua/dfllm/act.lua` (`squad_unequip`, unchanged)

## Actual
- r16d4 `commands.log`: `act.squad_release` every ~160 s with the same ids (1548313, 1564689, 1587545, ... alternating between two sets of 8-30 pieces); `kern.log` 'N gear pieces DF kept assigned to a squad position that does not wear them given back (5-12 for a vacant position or none, 14-25 unfetched for 2400 ticks by the soldier of an occupied one)' 122 + 31 times. `inspect what=military`: 'lying free but out of reach: 36 bin, 1 far, 24 alien, 22-29 reserved, 2-4 stuck' beside 'free usable body 12-22'.
- The squads have 10 positions each and 3-11 soldiers (gear: '11 soldiers'). `squad.uniform B melee` answered 'melee uniform on 10 positions'.
- No answer to 'why does soldier X lack piece Y': `inspect what=military` counted `empty` per category only.

## Root cause
`military.lua` `ensure_uniform` gave the FIRST uniform to the whole squad (`spec.positions` only when some position already had specs) and the `squad.uniform` verb looped over `s.positions` (all 10), so 6-7 vacant positions per squad (and the positions of dead or removed soldiers, which keep their specs) carried full uniform specs. DF reserves the free pieces for the specs of a vacant position exactly as for a soldier's (`items_assigned`, the BUG-598 'vac' count). With three squads that is up to 19 vacant positions x 5 armor slots claiming the 28 body armors; the soldiers of the later positions and squads (squad B) get what is left. `release_kit` (BUG-598) gave the vacant claims back, but the specs stayed, so DF reserved the pieces again at its next equipment update: the 160 s release loop.

## Fix
- `ensure_uniform`: `spec.positions = bare` always (the occupied positions without a spec); a squad.uniform success clears the retry backoff (before, a soldier seated inside the backoff waited for his specs); `ensure_uniform` runs again right after `fill` so a recruit seated in this upkeep has his specs in it.
- `squad.uniform` verb: vacant positions are skipped; `best_mats` is sized by the occupied positions to fill (3 soldiers, not 10 positions: a stock of 5 metal pieces now means metal), the answer says 'uniform on N positions' with N = the occupied ones.
- `M.vacant_sweep` (once per `REL_EVERY` 1,200 ticks, any mode): the vacant positions of A, B, C that still hold specs or assigned items are cleared with `act.squad_unequip` (the squads screen's remove uniform: specs deleted, assigned pieces back to `items_unassigned`); kern.log 'squad B: 7 vacant positions held uniform specs and the gear DF reserved for them: cleared'. A position gets its specs the upkeep it is occupied (`bare_positions`).
- Per-slot reason: `M.slot_why(K, member, cat, ctx)` / `M.slot_report(K, squad)`, in `inspect what=military` data `squads[].members[]` = `{unit, pos, slots = {cat = {code, why, item?}}}`, `squads[].why` = `{cat = {code = n}}`, `squads[].lack` = one line. Codes: `nospec` (no spec on the position), `assigned` (DF assigned piece N; the text says forbidden / owned / in a building / carried by unit N / in a forbidden bin / on another walk group / made by another race / claimed by N positions / unfetched for N ticks, else 'the soldier has not picked it up yet'), `mat` (spec metal or leather, 0 free of that class, N of other material lie free; refit moves the slot), `unlisted` (N free pieces lie there and DF assigned none), `none` (no free piece: the text counts clothes / forbidden / owned / bins / other walk group / other race / reserved / stuck / worn). Weapons use `weapon_detail`. The text of `inspect` is unchanged (the reply is cut at 300 characters).
