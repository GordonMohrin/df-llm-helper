## Root causes
1. `runner.lua Q.stuck_walls` (the BUG-044 fallback): the only guard was `seal.opening_ways` (`here_closable` or another opening with a door/bridge) from the last entrance check. That check counts openings between the map edge and the interior; it knows nothing of what stands outside. Every door spot of the seal could become a wall while the farm, the manager, the broker, the depot stood on the open side: the wall closed the last way to them.
2. Nothing looked at citizens or places already outside after a wall went up (CUTOFF/STRANDED only fire for missing food), so the wall was never questioned, and `bp.cancel` refuses a done project: no way back existed.
3. The `seal_stalled` decision offered `bp.place seal close=wall` for every floor-like piece ("recommended where the citizens keep another way") whatever the wall cut off.
4. `bp.place` of a seal with `close=wall` (CLI/follow) was never checked against what stands outside.
5. `appoint` could not replace a living holder: a manager outside the fort kept the post (`no vacant assignment`), and `manager_stuck` (BUG-450) did not know the case.

## Fix
- `seal.wall_cuts(tiles)`: a flood over the revealed walkable tiles of the citizens' main walk group, the given tiles closed (the project's other planned walls too), until every citizen on the group, farm plot, well, trade depot, workshop, furnace and office zone (`seal.reach_assets`) that stands on the group now is met or `bfs_max` tiles are visited; the places not met are `cuts`. Read-only, never yields.
- `runner.Q.wall_refuse` in `stuck_walls`: a wall that would cut something off (or whose safety cannot be proven: partial flood, citizens unknown) is not built; the door stays, kern.log says `door x,y,z stays a door: a wall would cut off N (farm 98,75,138, citizen ...)` once, and the door is made at the workshop (`economy.seal_doors`: a workshop task `ConstructDoor`, wood at the Carpenters or rock at the Masons, deduped against the queued tasks, none when a free door stands, the workshop request when no shop stands). No manager is needed for a workshop task.
- `runner.Q.place_walls`: `bp.place` of a seal with constructed-wall cells (`close=wall`, a `nodoor` opening) is refused with the cut places named and "close with doors" when the walls would cut something off (an unknown reach or a partial flood refuses too, as the fallback does; a `repair` document with walls is checked the same way, outside PEACE/RECOVERY only on a proven cut).
- `seal_stalled` (DECISION_NEEDED): where the wall was refused it offers no wall command; `how` says `no wall (...): the door is made at the workshop (task added); by hand: dfllm cmd order.add '{"job":"ConstructDoor","amount":2}'`.
- LOCKED_OUT: `runner.Q.lockout_scan` (daily, PEACE/RECOVERY) asks `seal.locked_out` about the walls of ours (`Q.own_walls`: the `sw` / `dw` records, the walls a finished project left (`S.rt.ow`, kept by `Q.keep_walls` because `forget()` dropped the records at PROJECT_DONE) and the built `Cw` cells of a running seal / repair document): citizens or places off the main walk group beside such a wall raise DECISION_NEEDED `locked_out` (A, again after 30 days) with `units`, `places`, `at`, `proj` and `cmd` = `dfllm cmd bp.cancel '{"proj":"<id>","reopen":"x,y,z;..."}'`. `bp.cancel reopen` takes down walls of ours (quickfort undo of `Cw` on those tiles; any other tile is refused; the project may be done; a document wall of a running project becomes a door cell); then `bp.place seal at=last` puts a door. wake.py `locked_out_hint`.
- The manager (`appoint MANAGER` refused 'no vacant assignment'): `economy.out_watch` (daily) keeps `p.out[MANAGER|BROKER]` while the holder stands off the citizens' groups (`snapshot.far`); `manager_stuck` fires after `OUT_DAYS` (7) days outside instead of 14 and says so (`d.out`, a reachable candidate, `d.replace` = `dfllm cmd appoint '{"code":"MANAGER","unit":N,"replace":true}'` as the first command); `appoint ... replace:true` takes the post from a holder who has stood outside for 7 days (never from one among the citizens; only MANAGER and BROKER; `act.appoint(code, unit, replace)` vacates the assignment and removes the old position link, then seats the new one); the plain refusal now says the holder lives and for how long he has been outside.
