## Cause (round-15 diagnosis, proven from the events)
The stage-2 relaxation of the bounded wait (`gate.lua` 65eca7bd `holders` l.2086 keeps only units with d == 0, l.2236 `lim = 0` for crossers, `control` l.3106 `M.approach(..., maxd = 0)`) removed the reach of 13 steps (13 x 9 = 117 ticks) that matched the pull-to-move window: a walker 1 to 13 steps out holds nothing at stage 2, the pull stays valid until the job vanishes, and after the job vanishes nothing is checked ('moving' only logs). Neither GATE_FORCED nor GATE_CRUSH_RISK `forced` appeared, so it was not the crush_forced path. Contributing: stage-2 cancels did not count toward `pull_cap`, so the retry loop never parked and ran 5 pulls in 585 ticks, each a lottery ticket against the crossing stream (four failed, the fifth passed). Three walkers were onto B1 at once at the ALERT start (#38026: soldier 27354 and citizen 30489 1 step out, citizen 26276 16 steps). Soldier 27323 stands on or near the footprint repeatedly. Any rule that raises with walkers within reach can crush one; any rule that waits for none never raises on a busy fort (the 3-6 tile ring is never quiet).

## What round 15 did and why it is withdrawn
`M.bound_stage` (stage 1 after 450 ticks, stage 2 after 1500) let walkers go in ALERT / RECOVERY / lockdown / PEACE lockdown. The live result is the crush above. It is removed completely: `bound_stage`, `BOUND_FOOT`/`BOUND_GRACE`, `r.bw`, the `maxd` of `M.approach`, the `pull_cap` exemption, GATE `bounded`. The stand-by exemption of BUG-906 (`M.stand_by`) is kept ONLY in SIEGE/BREACH (where the raise is forced anyway), the PEACE exemptions of BUG-905 are gone.

## Fix: the reach rule
Outside SIEGE/BREACH (ALERT, RECOVERY, PEACE and DRILL, second review of 15b: the drill's own timed rules let a pull stand when a walker was 14 or more steps out, past CROSS_REACH 13, with a lever latency of 1000; not a quiet PEACE raise, which has `peace_wait`; not the player's `lever:` want) `holders()` adds, at the end, every unit of the census scan (`sc.far`, `PATH_R` 24 tiles, the walkers on the levels beside the footprint too) that can reach the footprint before the bridge has moved (`M.reach_hold`): steps (the walk path DF shows when it enters the footprint, else the tiles between it and the footprint) x `REACH_TPT` (9 ticks a tile) <= `M.reach_ticks()` = max(the kernel's own measured lever latency `st.lat`, `REACH_FLOOR` 600) + `TIMED_WIN` (110: the bridge's own movement). Standing, working, walking elsewhere and a soldier at his station all count (a station 4 tiles out is 36 ticks away); a unit ON the footprint counts; tame animals only when their path enters it; the job's worker and the bridge's pullers in their room do not (as before). The pull is neither queued nor kept: control step (4) answers 'blocked' (GATE_FAIL after the deadline names them: `citizen 7 12 tiles away, can reach it in 108 ticks`), a pending pull is cancelled when one comes into reach. The units hold the raise until they are out of the census. Because the floor (600) alone covers 66 tiles, the census radius (24) decides in practice: no unit within 24 tiles. Not raised = not crushed.
- SIEGE/BREACH keep rounds 3 and 12 (timed force, bounded crush BUG-881; the crush is the accepted price there).
- The player's lever: `lever` up of an INNER bridge is allowed in ALERT and SIEGE without the emergency exit (contract `modes`, gate's verb), refused at once with the kernel's own refusal when a unit stands ON the footprint now (`lever up B1 refused: citizen 90 on the footprint (a raise would crush him; the want is not set, try again when the footprint is clear)`), outer / core bridges and lever down stay refused with their own text; once wanted, the player's want waits for units on / beside the footprint and walkers onto it like every pull but not for the reach rule (the player decides).
- Reporting: `GATE_CRUSH_RISK` (forced raise: `M.crush_forced`; moving bridge: `M.crush_risk`) names EVERY unit on the footprint, each once per pull / per move, a unit that steps on later in a further event; new fields `units`, `kinds`. y472 #37124 / #37135 said `k=citizen` for a bridge that crushed soldier 27055: the event named only the first unit on the footprint (citizen 26276) once per pull and the soldier stepped on later. The SIEGE raise itself is round 12's rule unchanged (the diagnosis does not show a unit on the footprint at the pull with a hold skipped by a defect: `late` clears the whole hold list by design).
