# BUG-907 An ALERT / RECOVERY / lockdown raise waits for walkers for ever ... and the bounded wait that replaced it crushed a citizen: the REACH rule
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested); final rule (15b, lead decision, option A): a kernel raise outside SIEGE / BREACH is pulled only when the footprint is provably clear for the whole window from pull to end of movement; the post-job gap is closed by the hold
Severity: high
Found: Run 17 i3 Orangering y469 autumn d30-31 and spring d66-67: ALERT, lone hostile, no ARRIVAL: GATE_FAIL `B1 not up after 132905 ticks ... blocked: soldier 26632 next to it (squad 99 on its way to its station 86,60,142), citizen 26661 moving 2 tiles from it, citizen 29130 walking onto it in 13 steps`; 8 kernel pulls cancelled for units walking toward it, DECISION gate_traffic #34301; raised only by the player's lever. Round 15 (kernel 7f3706e5, y473 autumn d4, mode ALERT, 1 hostile): GATE `bounded` #38027 stage 1 (waited 461), #38041 stage 2 (waited 1521), pulls #38028..#38040 and #38042..#38047, `GATE_CRUSH_RISK` #38051 `soldier 27323 on the footprint at 86,64,142`, `B1 moving` #38050 at 190919721, 117 ticks after the pull #38047 was queued, `BRIDGE_CRUSH` #38052 / DEATH #38053: citizen 29043 crushed (last seen 86,64,142 at t=190919721); CUTOFF #38061 (7 citizens cut off from drink) until the return window #38075; y473 winter d31 #38202: soldier 27323 on 86,64,142 again at the move (760 ticks pull-to-moving), no death. Also every lone-hostile ALERT ended in GATE_FAIL (attacks 6, 7, 9, 11, 15, 17) and the player's lever was refused 'lever not allowed in ALERT'.
Module: `lua/dfllm/gate.lua` (`M.reach_hold`, `M.reach_ticks`, `M.reach_applies`, `holders`, `M.crush_forced`, `M.crush_risk`, `M.verbs.lever`, `M.lever_on_footprint`), `lua/dfllm/util/contract.lua` + `dfpilot/schema.py` (`lever` modes)

## Cause (round-15 diagnosis, proven from the events)
The stage-2 relaxation of the bounded wait (`gate.lua` 65eca7bd `holders` l.2086 keeps only units with d == 0, l.2236 `lim = 0` for crossers, `control` l.3106 `M.approach(..., maxd = 0)`) removed the reach of 13 steps (13 x 9 = 117 ticks) that matched the pull-to-move window: a walker 1 to 13 steps out holds nothing at stage 2, the pull stays valid until the job vanishes, and after the job vanishes nothing is checked ('moving' only logs). Neither GATE_FORCED nor GATE_CRUSH_RISK `forced` appeared, so it was not the crush_forced path. Contributing: stage-2 cancels did not count toward `pull_cap`, so the retry loop never parked and ran 5 pulls in 585 ticks, each a lottery ticket against the crossing stream (four failed, the fifth passed). Three walkers were onto B1 at once at the ALERT start (#38026: soldier 27354 and citizen 30489 1 step out, citizen 26276 16 steps). Soldier 27323 stands on or near the footprint repeatedly. Any rule that raises with walkers within reach can crush one; any rule that waits for none never raises on a busy fort (the 3-6 tile ring is never quiet). The lead chose the second: safety beats availability.

## What round 15 did and why it is withdrawn
`M.bound_stage` (stage 1 after 450 ticks, stage 2 after 1500) let walkers go in ALERT / RECOVERY / lockdown / PEACE lockdown. The live result is the crush above. It is removed completely: `bound_stage`, `BOUND_FOOT`/`BOUND_GRACE`, `r.bw`, the `maxd` of `M.approach`, the `pull_cap` exemption, GATE `bounded`. The stand-by exemption of BUG-906 (`M.stand_by`) is kept ONLY in SIEGE/BREACH (where the raise is forced anyway), the PEACE exemptions of BUG-905 are gone.

## Fix: the reach rule
Outside SIEGE/BREACH (ALERT, RECOVERY, PEACE and DRILL, second review of 15b: the drill's own timed rules let a pull stand when a walker was 14 or more steps out, past CROSS_REACH 13, with a lever latency of 1000; not a quiet PEACE raise, which has `peace_wait`; not the player's `lever:` want) `holders()` adds, at the end, every unit of the census scan (`sc.far`, `PATH_R` 24 tiles, the walkers on the levels beside the footprint too) that can reach the footprint before the bridge has moved (`M.reach_hold`): steps (the walk path DF shows when it enters the footprint, else the tiles between it and the footprint) x `REACH_TPT` (9 ticks a tile) <= `M.reach_ticks()` = max(the kernel's own measured lever latency `st.lat`, `REACH_FLOOR` 600) + `TIMED_WIN` (110: the bridge's own movement). Standing, working, walking elsewhere and a soldier at his station all count (a station 4 tiles out is 36 ticks away); a unit ON the footprint counts; tame animals only when their path enters it; the job's worker and the bridge's pullers in their room do not (as before). The pull is neither queued nor kept: control step (4) answers 'blocked' (GATE_FAIL after the deadline names them: `citizen 7 12 tiles away, can reach it in 108 ticks`), a pending pull is cancelled when one comes into reach. The units hold the raise until they are out of the census. Not raised = not crushed.
- FINAL RULE (option A; the lead's decision, safety beats availability): the window W = `reach_ticks()` = max(measured lever latency, `REACH_FLOOR` 600) + `TIMED_WIN` 110 is a TIME in which any unit that is not an animal can walk onto the footprint, standing or walking: nothing stops a moving bridge. `M.reach_census` reads the whole fort census (`around(K, fp, steps, true)`, any level: the larger of the xy and the z distance is a lower bound of a walk) out to ceil(W / REACH_TPT) + 3 tiles (the 3 = a unit walks that far between two looks, FAST 25 ticks; the scan is cached per FAST ticks in `rt[name].rc`). EVERY non-animal unit of it whose steps to the footprint (the walk path DF shows, else the tiles between: a lower bound, so an unknown path holds) x 9 <= W holds the pull: standing or moving, a soldier at his station too, any distance inside the window. The idle-fort exemption of the third review ('past PATH_R only while MOVING') is gone: it WAS the post-job gap. The same list is `peace_blockers` of the quiet PEACE raise (beyond its own 20 tiles `peace_clear_r`: `M.reach_units`), so the quiet raise waits for the whole window too, and `peace_busy` cancels a pending quiet pull for any of them. Modes: ALERT, RECOVERY, PEACE (the quiet raise included), DRILL (`REACH_MODES`). A missing census, a census older than `REACH_FRESH` (600 ticks; sense refreshes every 100) or a bridge without a valid footprint holds: `hold.unknown`, the raise waits (fail closed).
- While a pull is pending the same rule cancels it (`holders` is called on the queued job every pass, FAST 25 ticks: a unit that enters the window, or starts to walk inside it, cancels the pull before the job is done). A unit that starts to walk AFTER the pull was queued was inside the window when it was queued (the window is >= 710 ticks, the nearest unit that can reach the footprint in that time was held), so the pull was never made: the post-job gap is closed.
- Reporting: the hold shows as the existing GATE wait line (`B1 up waits: citizen 910 30 tiles away, can reach it in 270 ticks`) and, at the deadline, GATE_FAIL `blocked` naming the nearest units; after `REACH_DAY` (1,200 ticks) of the same want ONE DECISION_NEEDED `gate_reach_traffic` says that the bridge stays down because of traffic (nearest unit and distance, number of units, the window; options reroute / relocate / lever) - `M.reach_day`, once per want. The quiet PEACE raise keeps its `peace_stuck` + `gate_peace_traffic`. The old warning `reach_resid` (a raise pulled with units past the scan) is gone.
- SIEGE/BREACH keep rounds 3 and 12 (timed force, bounded crush BUG-881; the crush is the accepted price there).
- The player's lever: `lever` up of an INNER bridge is allowed in ALERT and SIEGE without the emergency exit (contract `modes`, gate's verb), refused at once with the kernel's own refusal when a unit stands ON the footprint now (`lever up B1 refused: citizen 90 on the footprint (a raise would crush him; the want is not set, try again when the footprint is clear)`), outer / core bridges and lever down stay refused with their own text; once wanted, the player's want waits for units on / beside the footprint and walkers onto it like every pull but not for the reach rule (the player decides).
- Reporting: `GATE_CRUSH_RISK` (forced raise: `M.crush_forced`; moving bridge: `M.crush_risk`) names EVERY unit on the footprint, each once per pull / per move, a unit that steps on later in a further event; new fields `units`, `kinds`. y472 #37124 / #37135 said `k=citizen` for a bridge that crushed soldier 27055: the event named only the first unit on the footprint (citizen 26276) once per pull and the soldier stepped on later. The SIEGE raise itself is round 12's rule unchanged (the diagnosis does not show a unit on the footprint at the pull with a hold skipped by a defect: `late` clears the whole hold list by design).

## Cost per frame
The wide scan runs once per `FAST` (25) ticks per bridge and is cached in `rt[name].rc` (`around(K, fp, steps, true)` is one pass over the census ids); the frames in between (a DRILL frame is 9 ticks) reuse the list.

## Cost
Outside SIEGE / BREACH the kernel raises a bridge only when no non-animal unit of the fort can reach the footprint within the window (80 tiles at the 600 floor, 123 at latency 1000): on a busy fort the inner bridge STAYS DOWN, and in a fort that is 100 tiles across nearly always. The kernel says so (GATE wait line, GATE_FAIL naming the nearest unit and its distance, one DECISION_NEEDED `gate_reach_traffic` after 1,200 ticks); the player can raise it with the `lever` verb (ALERT / SIEGE, inner bridge, up, refused with a unit ON the footprint; the fair-play limits of the verb are unchanged). Arrival windows before the mode is SIEGE keep bridges down while units are in the window; SIEGE/BREACH take over with the timed force (unchanged, the crush there is the accepted price). The old BUG-587/758 releases (a citizen's recall stream does not hold an ALERT raise; a soldier who stands is let go) are withdrawn outside SIEGE/BREACH.
Limits that honestly REMAIN (the post-job gap itself is closed): (1) units that ENTER the map inside the window (a migrant wave, a caravan, a visitor from beyond the census) are not in the census when the pull is queued; the pull cancels for them only once a census pass has them (<= 100 ticks, 25 in ALERT), and a bridge already moving cannot be stopped. What the kernel says then: nothing before, GATE_CRUSH_RISK / BRIDGE_CRUSH after (reports as before). (2) A unit faster than `REACH_TPT` 9 ticks a tile (hurried, mounted, falling) covers the window's distance in less time than the rule assumes. (3) A lever latency longer than the measured one (`st.lat`, floor 600): a pull whose job takes longer than the window leaves the bridge moving after W. (4) The straight-line lower bound of a walk holds MORE than needed, never less. (5) SIEGE / BREACH keep their own bounded crush (BUG-881, rounds 3 and 12).

## Live-Erwartung
In ALERT / RECOVERY / PEACE (lockdown or quiet raise) / DRILL B1 is pulled only when no non-animal unit of the fort is within ~80..125 tiles of it (path unknown = tile distance); a busy fort keeps it down: GATE `B1 up waits: citizen X 30 tiles away, can reach it in 270 ticks`, GATE_FAIL `blocked` at the deadline, after 1,200 ticks ONE DECISION_NEEDED `gate_reach_traffic` (quiet PEACE: `peace_stuck` + `gate_peace_traffic`). BRIDGE_CRUSH by a kernel raise in ALERT / RECOVERY / PEACE / DRILL does not occur except for units entering the map inside the window or faster than 9 ticks a tile (limits 1, 2). The player's `lever bridge=B1 want=up` works in ALERT and SIEGE. NOT promised: that the inner bridge is up when the alarm comes on a busy fort (it is not: that is the price); that nobody is crushed in SIEGE/BREACH.
