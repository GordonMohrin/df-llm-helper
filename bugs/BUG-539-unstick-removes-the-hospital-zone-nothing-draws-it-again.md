# BUG-539 water.unstick removed the hospital zone and nothing drew it again; the site search then found no hospital site
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested); open: the restore is not proven against DF (act.hospital_restore is the zones screen's add zone done through quickfort plus a location attach)
Severity: high (a fort with patients and no hospital zone: no beds, no care rules, G3 red for 15+ days)
Found: 2026-11-06, Run 16b i2 Brotherarrow y395 spr d50 -> y396 spr d20 (`runs/run-16b/FR-BUGS.md` "BUG (R32) water.unstick loescht die Hospital-Zone und sie kommt nicht wieder"; events.jsonl #5091 HOSPITAL_REMOVED `stalled`, kern.log `hospital none`, cli.log `bp.sites hospital` ERR; read only)
Module: `lua/dfllm/water.lua` (`M.remember`, `M.restore`, verb `hospital.restore`, `remove_dry`, `stall_hospital`), `lua/dfllm/act.lua` (`hospital_restore`), `dfpilot/bp/sites.py`, `dfpilot/cmd.py`, `dfpilot/schema.py`, `lua/dfllm/util/contract.lua`

## Actual
`cmd water.unstick unit=26367` ended in `stall_hospital`: "removed hospital zone(s) 18030: a GiveWater job stalled, patient 26897 at thirst 49384" (events #5091). The patient drank within a minute (thirst 44k -> 25k). Afterwards `status` said "hospital none", `inspect hospital` gaps `no_zone`, 6-10 patients (a migrant wave and a fight) lay without a zone for 15+ days (kern.log: "hospital none ... 6 patients", "0 hospital zone(s): GiveWater needs water, not a zone"). `bp sites hospital` (also `--fresh`) ended in "no valid site on the snapshot (nearest accepted water: zone 17696 ...)"; earlier it offered S1 117,125,137.

## Root cause
- `water.lua` `stall_hospital` (the third ladder rung, also run by `water.unstick`) and `remove_dry` (BUG-034) call `K.act.hospital_remove` (`act.lua` `I.hospital_remove`: `dfhack.buildings.deconstruct(zone)`) and keep nothing: no rectangle, no location id, no way back. No module draws a hospital zone, and the only other way (`bp.place tpl=hospital`) builds a NEW location with new furniture on a free site.
- The lost S1 is not a change of the water rule: the error is the empty candidate list BEFORE the water filter (`sites._near_water` would say "no site within N tiles"). In cli.log the follow loop placed `tombs` on S1 twice (y395, 1793937073 and 1793937829) and the old hospital room with its beds still stood, so the free ground the earlier ranking had was used up. The logs do not prove which tile the tombs took; what is certain is that a second hospital was neither possible nor wanted (the first room stood) and no command named the way back.

## Fix
- `water.M.remember(p, h, now, why)`: both removal paths store `{id, loc, x1..y2, z, t, why 'dry'|'stall'}` in `p.hrm` (persisted, at most 12).
- `act.hospital_restore(spec)`: quickfort zone mode draws a meeting zone `m{name=Hospital}(WxH)` over the old rectangle (no `location` key: quickfort's `set_location` would open a SECOND location), then attaches it to the existing HOSPITAL location (`location_id`, `site_id`, `uncategorize`/`categorize(true)`, the location's `contents.building_ids`). Idempotent ('present' when a zone of that location covers exactly that rectangle); a failed attach deconstructs the new zone. Contract: `ACT.hospital_restore = {'water'}`.
- `water.M.restore` (each water run): a `stall` zone comes back once `restore_wait` (half a day) passed and no patient is over `patient_thirst` (25,000), at the latest after `restore_max` (5 days); a `dry` zone (BUG-034: no accepted water) as soon as an accepted source is within `near` of it. Not in the same pass on purpose: the removal helps because the patient is no hospital patient for a while. A hospital location that is gone drops the memory. `hosp_restore = false`: only the verb.
- Verb `hospital.restore {loc?}` (any mode): all remembered zones (or those of one location) at once; the unstick reply names it.
- State `water.hrm` (count of remembered zones). `bp sites hospital` / `bp.place hospital` (`sites.rank`, also the hand-placed anchor check) refuse while `hrm > 0`: "the fort's hospital room still stands, only its zone was removed ... `dfllm cmd hospital.restore` ... no second hospital until then" instead of "no valid site".
- `gate.lua` untouched.
