# BUG-774 a bridge whose levers all lie outside Kern+ cannot be raised in time: the fort falls when the bridge never rises
Status: fixed (offline, live-untested)
Severity: high
Found: 2026-11-11, Run 16a i1 Basementflew y544 aut d82 / win d2 (kern 87b10464; FR-BUGS '## BUG 2. Ueberraschungs-Belagerung' and its Nachtrag): the 2nd surprise siege (Cave Dragon 36093 + goblins, ambush, snatcher), pop 52 -> 7
Module: `lua/dfllm/gate.lua` (`lever_outside`, `lever_home`, `home_step`, `home_tiles`, `home_covers`, `allowed`), `lua/dfllm/act.lua` (`lever_build`), `lua/dfllm/runner.lua` (`add_levers`), `lua/dfllm/readiness.lua` (R0 `lever_outside`, `ask_leverout`)

## Actual
The manifest of the fort lists B1's two levers at (37,97,129) and (36,97,129), 5-6 tiles west of its footprint (42,100..102), outside Kern+ (`burrows`: only `Kern+` and `dfllm-levers`, no refuge). ARRIVAL #29746 / #29751: 'recall: 35 outside Kern+', after 1,915 ticks still 11 outside; the pullers of the lever room were chosen 60 tiles away (kern.log 'lever pullers dfllm-levers = {26740,35917}', each 'let go ... away' after 600+ ticks, a new far pair each time); B1 was raised only after 1,650 ticks in the 1st siege (y540), and at y544 GATE_FAIL #29924 'B1 not up after 909 ticks (no worker: its levers lie outside the alert burrow Kern+)' -> BREACH #29882, pop 52 -> 14 -> 7. BUG-765 (round 50) judges the race to such a lever room and, when the hostile is there first, leaves the lever burrow out of the alert: the bridge then stays down on purpose.

## Root cause
`gate.lua`: a pull is only possible for a citizen the civ alert lets walk to the lever. With every linked lever outside Kern+ and the refuge there is no lever the alert's own burrow holds (`lever_in`, `order()` class 1), so each siege is a race over the open ground (`race_lost`, `fill`, `lever_room`), won or lost by where the citizens happened to work. Nothing in PEACE noticed it: readiness knew `levers:<B>` (fewer than 2 linked), `refuge_levers` (none in the refuge, only with a refuge in the manifest) and `bridge_levers` (none linked at all), but not 'all of them outside the walls'. The manifest had a lever room (`dfllm-levers`) outside Kern+ and `allowed()` / `duty()` still sent pullers to it.

## Fix
1. `gate.lever_outside(K)` (`gate.lua`, public): the managed bridges whose linked levers ALL lie outside Kern+ and the refuge: `{[bridge] = {n, at}}`. `gate.lever_home(K)` adds what gate does about each (`cause` designated / no_tile / refused / no_act / unread).
2. `home_step` (`gate.lua`, from `M.step`, PEACE only, every `HOME_EVERY` 600 ticks): per such bridge ONE lever (`HOME_N`) on a free Kern+ floor tile: `home_tiles` takes the tiles of Kern+ within `HOME_R` (20) tiles of the bridge and its old levers on the bridge's level, at least `HOME_MARGIN` (4) tiles from EVERY bridge footprint of that level, revealed plain floor (`snapshot.tile` '.', nothing built or designated, no liquid), at least 3 of its 4 neighbours plain floor (no corridor, no door passage), nearest to the bridge's first old lever first (a lever links over any distance: it need not lie near the bridge), at most `HOME_READS` (400) tile reads. `act.lever_build` (new, owner gate: quickfort build mode key `Tl`, as `rescue_ramp_build`) designates it, `runner.add_levers` (new, owner runner: appends the tile to `manifest.bridges.<B>.levers`, no duplicates, at most 12) lists it, and gate's own link pass (`links`) links it like any manifest lever once the mechanism is there (1 mechanism for the lever, 2 for the link). A lever listed and still not on its tile after `HOME_REDO` (12 game days) is designated again. Never in a lockdown, SIEGE, BREACH, DRILL or ALERT. With the lever linked the bridge leaves the list; `order()` already pulls a lever on the alert's burrow first.
3. `home_covers` (`gate.lua`, in `allowed`): a lever room that lies outside Kern+ and the refuge and whose bridges all have a linked lever inside Kern+ / the refuge has no duty (`pullers_step` lets its pullers go, `fill` sends nobody) and stays out of the civ alert (`gate.rooms`): nobody walks out to the attack side when a lever inside exists.
4. readiness: R0 flag `lever_outside` (PEACE; outside PEACE the last answer stands), state `ready.lo` (bridges), the status line `LEVERS OUTSIDE Kern+ on ...`, DECISION_NEEDED `lever_outside` (A) once per `DEF.ask_days` (30) with `cause` designated (gate builds its own: waits for a mechanism, the link for 2 more) / no_tile (`cmds` `bp.place tpl=fortcore site=same stage=3 retrofit=3`, the fortcore lever strip lies in Kern+) / refused (`inspect what=defense`), `n` free mechanisms. `GATE_FAIL` no_worker / outside_alert / slow adds '; its levers lie outside Kern+ (R0 lever_outside)'.
5. fortcore already places its main levers in the Z3 / Z4 strips inside Kern+ (`fortcore.LEVER_ROOMS`, `k.ent(-4, 35, 5, 41, ...)`); nothing changes there.

Decision, siege-time fallbacks not built: no designated crew of armed soldiers (`unfit()` keeps soldiers out of the pullers: a squad on duty takes no PullLever job, which cannot be shown offline) and no extension of Kern+ over the lever tiles (the alert would then hold the civilians at levers outside the walls, the y540 / y544 deaths). The fix is the lever inside the walls, built while it is quiet.

## Open
Until the new lever is built and linked (a mechanism and 2 for the link, a Mechanic) the bridge still depends on the BUG-765 race. A fort without mechanisms (no hard stone) waits; the question names it (`n`, cause). A lever the kernel designates can be refused by DF (no material); then `refused`. The tile choice is a heuristic (floor, 3 open neighbours); a lever in a room the player wanted free has to be moved by hand. The chosen tile lies on the bridge's own level only. Live behaviour of `Tl` through quickfort is the same call `bp` fortcore makes, but not run here.
